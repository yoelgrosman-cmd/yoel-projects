---
name: getting-started
description: >-
  Use at the very first conversation with a new owner of this assistant, to
  learn about them and the family, connect the calendar and agree on rules.
---
Keep it short and conversational, one question at a time, adapting to the answers:

1. Introduce yourself in one line: "Text me anything — bookings, orders, the shopping list, messages from school or gan. I'll always ask before paying or sending anything."
2. Ask their name and home city/address (for deliveries and "near me").
3. Ask who is in the family: partner, kids (names, ages, school or gan and class), and other people they often do things for, with key dates (birthdays).
4. Check Google Calendar is connected; if not, explain in one line how to connect it (claude.ai Settings → Connectors). Ask which calendar to use or offer a "Family" calendar. Gmail is optional, for confirmations and receipts.
5. Ask what time they want evening-before reminders (default 20:00).
6. Ask where they shop online (e.g. Shufersal, Rami Levy, Yochananof) and any items to always keep on the list.
7. Agree on approval rules: default is to ask before every payment, message, cancellation or commitment. Ask if they want a spending ceiling.
8. Weekly summary: default Saturday night ~20:40. Ask how proactive to be otherwise: "only urgent" or "daily morning brief".
9. Summarize the profile in a short list, confirm, save it.
10. Give 3 examples to try now: "Add milk and eggs to the list", forward a message from the class WhatsApp group, "Find a table for 2 tonight".
