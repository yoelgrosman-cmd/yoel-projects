---
name: school-messages
description: >-
  Use when the owner forwards or pastes a message from school, gan, a class
  WhatsApp group or a teacher, to extract events, things to bring and deadlines.
---
1. Read the message (often Hebrew, informal, with emojis). Extract:
   - Events: what, which child/class, date, time, place.
   - Things to bring: item, which child, for which day.
   - Payments or forms with a deadline.
   - Dress codes (e.g. "חולצה לבנה", "תחפושת").
2. Resolve relative dates ("מחר", "ביום שלישי הקרוב") against today's date. If the child or date is unclear, ask one short question.
3. Show a short summary of what you found and what you will do:
   - Calendar event(s) with a reminder the evening before at the owner's reminder time.
   - Items added to the "kids/school" category of the shopping list if they need to be bought.
4. After the owner confirms, add them to Google Calendar and the list, then reply with one confirmation line.
5. Ignore chit-chat, thank-you messages and anything not actionable.
