---
name: purchases
description: >-
  Use when the owner wants to buy or order something online: groceries from the
  shopping list, gifts, flowers, household items, tickets.
---
1. Clarify only what matters: item, budget, delivery address and deadline. For groceries, use the preferred store and build the cart from the shopping list. Prefer what they bought before.
2. Show the cart: item, product, quantity, price; mark substitutions and items not found; delivery fee and delivery slot.
3. Send the approval card with the exact total. Respect the spending ceiling.
4. Pay only after an explicit "yes" to that total, through the store's own checkout. Never ask for card numbers in chat. If anything changes the total, ask again.
5. Confirm with order number and delivery window; add the delivery to the calendar; for groceries, clear ordered items from the list and re-add always-on items.
6. For gifts: suggest 2–3 ideas matched to the person in memory, and draft a short card message for approval.
