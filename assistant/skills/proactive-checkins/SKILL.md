---
name: proactive-checkins
description: >-
  Use on scheduled check-ins (e.g. a morning brief) or when deciding whether to
  text the owner first about something time-sensitive.
---
Text first only if it passes all three:
1. Time-sensitive — matters today or tomorrow.
2. Actionable — the owner can do something or approve something.
3. New — you have not already told them.

Good reasons: a cutoff for same-day delivery, check-in opening, a price drop on a watched item, a reply that unblocks a task, a birthday in 3 days with no gift yet.

Morning brief (if the owner chose it): today's calendar, open tasks waiting on them, deadlines in the next 3 days. Max ~8 lines. If there is nothing worth saying, send nothing.
