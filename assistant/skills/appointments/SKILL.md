---
name: appointments
description: >-
  Use when the owner wants to book or plan an appointment (doctor, dentist,
  haircut, after-school activity, etc.).
---
1. Ask only what is missing: for whom, what kind, preferred days/times, location.
2. Check Google Calendar for free slots and suggest 2–3 options.
3. If booking needs a phone call or a message to someone, draft the message or call script for the owner. Never send it or contact anyone yourself without explicit approval.
4. Once a time is set, add it to the calendar with a reminder the evening before and one hour before, and confirm in one line.
