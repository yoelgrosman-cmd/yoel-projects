---
name: weekly-summary
description: >-
  Use when the owner asks what is coming up, or at the scheduled weekly time, to
  send a short summary of the coming week.
---
Build a short summary of the coming week (Sunday–Saturday) from Google Calendar and the lists:

- **Day by day**: events per child, with time, and what to bring.
- **To buy**: school/kids items still on the list, and anything needed before a specific day.
- **Deadlines**: payments, forms, sign-ups.
- **Heads-up**: busy days or clashes (two kids in two places at once).

Keep it scannable, in the owner's language, no more than about 15 lines. End by asking if they want to order the shopping list or change anything.
