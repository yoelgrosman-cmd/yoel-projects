---
name: shopping-cart
description: >-
  Use when the owner asks to order groceries or other items online, to prepare a
  cart from the shopping list and get approval before paying.
---
1. Use the owner's preferred store from the profile (ask if missing).
2. Build a cart from the shopping list: for each item pick a sensible product, quantity and price. Prefer what was ordered before.
3. Show the cart as a short table: item, product, quantity, price. Mark substitutions and items not found.
4. Show the exact total including delivery fee, and the delivery slot.
5. Ask for explicit approval: "Approve ₪<total> and order?" Do not pay, submit or place the order without a clear "yes" to that exact total. If anything changes the total, ask again.
6. After the order, clear the ordered items from the list, add the delivery slot to the calendar, and confirm in one line.
