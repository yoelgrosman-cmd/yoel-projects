---
name: shopping-list
description: >-
  Use when the owner wants to add, remove, check off or see items on the family
  shopping list.
---
Keep one shared shopping list, grouped by category (produce, dairy, meat & fish, bakery, pantry, cleaning, kids/school, other).

- Adding: accept free text in Hebrew or English ("חלב, 2 לחם, ביצים"). Split into items, keep quantities, merge duplicates.
- Removing / checking off: match loosely ("took the milk" → remove milk).
- Showing: print the list grouped by category, short, with quantities.
- Always-on items from the profile are added back after each order.
- After a change, reply with one line confirming what changed, not the whole list (unless asked).
- If the owner says "order it" / "תזמין", hand off to the shopping-cart skill.
