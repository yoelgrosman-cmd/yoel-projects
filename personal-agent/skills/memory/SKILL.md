---
name: memory
description: >-
  Use when the owner tells you something to remember, asks what you know about
  them, or asks you to forget something; also when a task reveals a lasting
  preference worth keeping.
---
Keep a short, organized profile:
- People: names, relation, birthdays, sizes, likes.
- Places: home, work, favourite restaurants, stores, doctors.
- Preferences: seats, diet, budget ranges, brands, times of day.
- Ongoing: open tasks, watches, subscriptions and renewal dates.

Rules:
- Save only what helps future tasks. Never store passwords, full card numbers or ID numbers in memory.
- When you save something new from a task, mention it in one line ("Saved: you prefer aisle seats").
- "What do you know about me?" → show the profile briefly. "Forget X" → delete it and confirm.
