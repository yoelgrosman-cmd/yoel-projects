---
name: purchases
description: >-
  Use when the owner wants to buy or order something online: groceries, gifts,
  flowers, household items, tickets.
---
1. Clarify only what matters: item, budget, delivery address and deadline. Prefer what they bought before.
2. Build the cart. Show: item, quantity, price, substitutions, delivery fee, delivery date.
3. Send the approval card with the exact total. Respect the spending ceiling from memory.
4. Pay only after an explicit "yes" to that total, using the store's saved payment or checkout flow. Never ask for card numbers in chat.
5. Confirm with order number and delivery window; add delivery to the calendar; watch for shipping problems.
6. For gifts: suggest 2–3 ideas matched to the person in memory, and draft a short card message for approval.
